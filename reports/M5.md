## What was built
- `PATCH /api/staff/space/[id]`: one of `hours`, `access`, `prices` or `confirm` per request; the whole document is re-validated on every write.
- Staff portal, Space info tab: hours per weekday (or "Not listed"), access and note per audience, price and unit per material, and a Confirm button on each of the five groups. Trainings and equipment can be confirmed but not edited here.

## Decisions to review
- Editing a group does not change its Estimated or Confirmed state; only pressing Confirm does. A confirmed group that is later edited stays confirmed with its old date.
- There is no "un-confirm" button.
