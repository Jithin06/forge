# Demo video script: 3 minutes

First draft, following SPEC.md section 9.1. Record on a real phone. Keep it under 3 minutes: the limit is hard.

**Before recording (do not skip):**
- The app must be on a live AI model. No "Sample data" banner may appear anywhere in the video (SPEC 4.1). As of this draft the app is still on sample data: see NEEDS.md items 1 and 2.
- Use real Google sign-in if it is ready. If you record with test sign-in, the gold "Test sign-in" banner will be visible; say plainly that sign-in is a test account in the prototype and that USC single sign-on is the production path.
- Post at least one real leftover piece with a real photo, so the plan has something free in it.
- Do not confirm prices on camera unless they are real. Confirming my estimated prices would label guesses as "Confirmed by staff".
- Every number you say must be in `RESULTS.md` that day.

| Time | Show | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 to 0:20 | A sketch on paper. A bin of offcuts at the Maker Space. | New students have ideas and no map. The tools are here at USC, spread across schools with different rules. And usable material gets thrown away. |
| 0:20 to 0:55 | Phone, Forge home page. Type the idea the way a beginner would: "something to hold my phone up on my desk so I can watch videos, wood maybe". Tap "Add a photo or sketch" and take the photo. Pick "I am a..." and "I have...". Tap "Find how to build it". Answer the tap questions. Tap "Show my plan". | You do not need the vocabulary. Say it in your own words and add a sketch. The AI asks only what changes the answer. |
| 0:55 to 1:30 | Scroll the plan slowly: the "Cheapest" and "Easiest for a first build" labels, the space and its badge, trainings, materials with the cost, "Free with scrap", then "Your first visit". | Here is the plan. Where to go. Whether you are allowed in. What training it takes. What it costs, added up from the space's own prices, and marked estimated where they are. And a checklist for your first visit. |
| 1:30 to 1:45 | Tap "Change my answers". Change "I am a..." to another audience. Run it again. Show the badge change. | Same idea, different student, different answer. Code decides who is allowed where, not the model. |
| 1:45 to 2:10 | On the free piece, tap "Claim this piece". Sign in. Land back on the plan showing "Claimed. Pick it up at... by...". | This part is free, because someone posted their leftovers. Sign-in keeps claims to real USC students. It is held for 48 hours. |
| 2:10 to 2:40 | Staff portal on the phone. "Post scrap": take a photo of an offcut. Show the AI draft in the form, fix one word, tap "Publish to the scrap bin". Then the "Space info" tab and a "Confirm" button, and the space page chip. | Staff post a leftover in seconds: one photo, the AI drafts the listing, they check it and publish. And they own their data. When they confirm it, "Estimated" becomes "Confirmed by staff". |
| 2:40 to 3:00 | One slide: the spaces listed, the eval score with its case count, tokens per plan, pieces reused. Only numbers from `RESULTS.md`. | A professor can hand this link to a first-year class. We are asking for one pilot semester with the Maker Space. |

## Shot list to prepare

## If something fails on camera
- "Forge is busy, try again in a minute": the free AI quota was hit. Wait a minute and retake; what you typed is kept.
- A plan with no free piece: post a leftover in the same material first (plywood for the phone stand).
