## Test sign-in
- `AUTH_MODE=test` turns on a picker on `/sign-in`: test USC student, test Maker Space staff, non-USC account. A gold "Test sign-in" banner shows on every page.
- With any other value: `POST /auth/test` is a 404, a test cookie is ignored, and neither the banner nor the picker is drawn (checked on a second server in google mode, in a browser).
- The test accounts are real rows in Supabase Auth (`forge-test-student@usc.edu`, `forge-test-staff@usc.edu`) because plans and claims reference users. They cannot receive email or sign in any other way.
