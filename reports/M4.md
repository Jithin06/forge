## What was built
- `describeScrap` with code-enforced rules (low confidence becomes unknown plastic or other and is flagged; title 6 words at most; size marked approximate).
- Routes: `GET /api/scrap`, `POST /api/scrap/[id]/claim`, `DELETE /api/claims/[id]`, `POST /api/staff/scrap/draft`, `POST` and `GET /api/staff/scrap`, `PATCH /api/staff/scrap/[id]`.
- `/scrap`: photo grid, material and space filters, item sheet, Claim. Signed out, Claim goes to sign-in and returns to the same item, where the claim finishes by itself.
- Plan page: free pieces with photo and Claim; "Just claimed" if someone else was faster, then the plan reloads to offer the next match.
- Staff portal: Post scrap (photo, AI draft, editable form, Publish) and Claims ("Picked up", "Release", "Remove").
- Impact counter on the home page, counted in SQL.
- `npm run clean:test` removes anything posted by test accounts or check scripts.
